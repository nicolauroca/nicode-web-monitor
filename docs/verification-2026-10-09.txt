Development increment: explicit inventory read from the enrolled-sites panel.

Check uses POST with Joomla CSRF validation and core.manage permission. It reads
only the selected site, decrypts its credential internally, and uses the existing
bounded HTTPS client with pinned address and identity verification. No automatic
retries, remote mutations, history or background polling are introduced.

The result shows section availability, collection/receipt timestamps and partial
status. Failed inventory reads do not imply that the website is offline. Reloading
the registry discards the result. Site credentials are never rendered.

Verification on disposable Joomla 6: 26 registry checks, 9 administrator HTTP
checks, 4 registry-to-TLS integration scenarios and 39 existing transport checks.
TLS integration covers complete/partial responses, revocation and wrong identity,
using a simulated connector and real encrypted registry storage (rolled back).
This is NOT verification against two independent Joomla installations. That
milestone remains pending, as do persisted observations, alerts and maintenance.

The initial TLS fixture omitted Content-Length and caused an SSL EOF; fixed the
fixture framing without weakening certificate checks or production transport.
